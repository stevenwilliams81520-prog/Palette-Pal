Detail

Fine and Extra fine keep small details like eyes, highlights and edges, but give you more, smaller areas to paint. For the most detail, use Extra fine with 25 or more colours and smoothing at 0.

Choose a photo to start. Simple pictures with clear shapes, like a pet, a landscape or a bowl of fruit, make the most paintable patterns.
